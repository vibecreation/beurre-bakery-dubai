OUR STORY

A corner of France, warehouse-set

Set inside a converted warehouse in the heart of Al Khawaneej, Beurre brings the aroma of fresh butter and laminated dough to a quiet corner of Dubai. Every croissant is folded by hand, every espresso pulled with care, in a room designed to feel unhurried — warm wood, soft light, and the kind of stillness that makes you want to stay for a second cup.
